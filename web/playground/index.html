Each card shows the expected result. ✓ Rejected banners should vanish within a second. ! Needs you ones must stay, with a note from Pip saying nothing was accepted.

Tip: banners remember your choice through cookies, just like real sites. Use Reset demo cookies to see them again. To test “remember my choice”, reload a page after it has been handled: there’s no banner and the popup says Your “no” is remembered.
