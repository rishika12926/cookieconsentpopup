Cookie pop-ups, politely declined.

CookieShield clicks “Reject” for you, checks that the site actually recorded your “no”, and tells you when it can’t. It never clicks “Accept” just to make a banner go away.

Install in two minutes

CookieShield isn’t in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it yourself. The install guide shows exactly where to click, with a picture for every step. It also works in Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers.

Open the step-by-step guide

💻 Laptop & desktop only · Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc · not available on phones or iPads

  1. Unzip the download. You’ll get a folder called cookieshield-extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode: the small switch in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the cookieshield-extension folder.
  4. Pin Pip from the puzzle-piece menu. A ✓ on the icon means a banner was rejected; a ! means it needs you.
  5. Try it on the test bench: 59 practice websites with every kind of cookie pop-up, so you can watch it work.

Honest about what happened

Most banner blockers just hide the pop-up, or quietly click whatever makes it disappear. CookieShield gives every visit one of these results, and keeps a history you can search.

Your history stays with you

History and site memory live only in your browser. No account, no ads, no tracking. By default CookieShield sends a daily count (banners met, rejected, problems): numbers only, no websites, no ID. You can switch it off. Sharing which websites show banners is opt-in. Privacy policy

Translation on your device

“Read this page in English” uses Chrome’s built-in on-device translator. Page text never leaves your computer.

Reports only if you choose

Problem reports are off by default. When you send one, it contains the site name and button labels — never the full address, cookies or page content.
