Install CookieShield

About 2 minutes. Follow the pictures and tick each step as you go.

💻 Laptop & desktop only · Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc · not available on phones or iPads

You’re on a phone or tablet. CookieShield can’t be installed on phones or iPads: Chrome extensions only work on a laptop or desktop. Open this page there: cookieconsentpopup.vercel.app/install

This doesn’t look like Chrome. CookieShield works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. Open this page in one of them.

1

Download it

Download CookieShieldDownload on your laptop. This won’t work on a phone or iPad.

On a Mac: open your Downloads folder and double-click cookieshield-extension.zip. You’ll get a folder called cookieshield-extension.

On Windows: open your Downloads folder, right-click cookieshield-extension.zip and choose Extract All → Extract. You’ll get a folder called cookieshield-extension.

Open your Downloads folder and unzip cookieshield-extension.zip. You’ll get a folder called cookieshield-extension.

2

Open Chrome’s extensions page

Chrome doesn’t let websites open this page for you, so copy the address, paste it into the address bar at the top, and press Enter:

chrome://extensionsCopied!

Or use the menu: click ⋮ (top right of Chrome) → Extensions → Manage Extensions.

3

Switch on “Developer mode”

Look at the top-right corner of the extensions page. There’s a small switch called Developer mode. Click it so it turns blue.

Don’t see it? Make the Chrome window wider; it’s always in the top-right corner. Developer mode only lets you add extensions from your computer. It doesn’t change anything else.

4

Click “Load unpacked” and pick the folder

Three new buttons appear on the left. Click Load unpacked, go to your Downloads, click the cookieshield-extension folder once, and press Select (or Open).

Shortcut: drag the cookieshield-extension folder straight onto the extensions page.

5

Pin Pip and try it

A welcome page opens by itself. Click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome and the pin next to CookieShield, so Pip sits in your toolbar.

Then open any website with a cookie pop-up, or the test bench. A tick on Pip means your “no” was sent and checked.

Something went wrong?
  • “Manifest file is missing or unreadable”: you picked the wrong folder. Pick the cookieshield-extension folder itself, the one that has manifest.json inside. Not the zip, and not your Downloads folder.
  • Developer mode is greyed out: your work or school manages this browser and blocks it. Use a personal Chrome profile instead.
  • Nothing happens on a site: reload the page once after installing. If a banner still isn’t handled, report the site.
