      How it works & privacy

      1

      Finds the banner

      Dedicated rules for 30 consent platforms (OneTrust, Cookiebot, Didomi, Usercentrics, Quantcast, Sourcepoint…), including ones hidden in Shadow DOM or iframes. Anything else is read by meaning in 25+ languages.

      2

      Only ever says “no”

      It clicks reject / “necessary only”, or switches every optional category off and saves. It never clicks Accept, OK or Subscribe. Then it checks the site’s own consent record. If it can’t reject safely, it tells you.

      3

      Keeps sites working

      After handling it checks scrolling, clicking, links and forms. It only repairs leftovers of the banner itself (like a stuck scroll lock), and you can undo or pause per site.

      What the statuses mean

      Privacy

      • History, stats and site memory are stored only in this browser.
      • No analytics, no tracking, no account. Nothing is sent unless you report a problem with sharing switched on.
      • Necessary cookies (sign-in, basket, security) are never touched. CookieShield never deletes cookies.
      • Translation uses Chrome’s on-device translator; page text stays on your computer.
