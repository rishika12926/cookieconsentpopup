Hi, I’m Pip. Cookie pop-ups are my job now.

I reject optional cookies for you, check the site really recorded your “no”, and tell you when I can’t. I never click “Accept”.

What I share with the person who makes me

Anonymous usage counts (on — you can switch it off)

Once a day: how many cookie banners I met, how many I rejected, how many I couldn’t, how often you stepped in (paused a site, reported a problem…), errors, how long it took, and the CookieShield and Chrome version. Just numbers — no website names, no addresses, no ID that could tell you apart from anyone else.

Which websites show cookie banners (off — only if you want to help)

Once a day: the website names only (like “example.com”) where a cookie banner appeared, which consent tool it used and whether I managed. It helps fix the sites that don’t work yet. Website names can say something about you, so this stays off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off again any time — anything not yet sent is deleted.

Never sent: full web addresses, page titles, searches, anything you type, cookies, sign-ins, page content, or your browsing history. Problem reports are only sent when you press “Report a problem” and choose to share.

You can see exactly what was sent, and change these choices, in Dashboard → Settings → Privacy & data. Privacy policy
